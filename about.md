I'm a master's student at Hof University of Applied Sciences finishing my thesis on
generative AI for automated product photography. My work sits at the intersection of
AI/ML, computer vision, and practical software development for e-commerce.

## research interests

- Generative models for product imagery (diffusion, GPT-Image, Gemini)
- Virtual try-on (VTON) with physics-aware garment representations
- Pattern-first 3D garment recovery from photographs
- Differentiable simulation for end-to-end clothing pipelines

## background

- **M.Sc.**, Hof University of Applied Sciences (in progress)
- **B.Sc.**, prior work on 3D virtual try-on with physics simulation
